Two ways to start learning

Start with a question or bring your own learning material. Both paths use the same Feynman learning cycle: understand, explain, find gaps, rebuild, and apply.

Mode 1

Learn a concept

Ask something like, “What is quantum physics? Explain it to a complete beginner.” The assistant gives a clear explanation first, defines unfamiliar terms, and uses examples before inviting you to teach the idea back.

Mode 2

Learn from your material

Bring a textbook, PDF, article, webpage, lecture transcript, notes, photograph, or diagram. The assistant explains the accessible source in plain language, keeps its meaning intact, and returns to relevant passages when repairing gaps.

The Feynman learning cycle

The goal is not to memorise a polished answer. It is to build an explanation you can recreate and use independently.

01 · UnderstandStart with a simple, accurate explanation.
02 · Teach backExplain the idea in your own words.
03 · Find gapsSpot jargon, vague wording, and missing steps.
04 · RebuildRepair one important gap at a time.
05 · Explain againReconstruct the idea independently.
06 · ApplyTest understanding with a new example.

Built around genuine understanding

A learner may recognise a term or repeat a definition without understanding what it means. This skill asks the assistant to notice that difference.

  • Beginner-friendly explanations before teach-back
  • Technical terms explained in ordinary language
  • Specific diagnosis instead of generic praise
  • One important knowledge gap repaired at a time
  • Adaptive pacing for beginners and advanced learners
  • Clear separation between source claims and outside context

Frequently asked questions

What is the Feynman Technique?

It is a learning approach built around explaining an idea in simple language, noticing where the explanation breaks down, returning to the missing knowledge, and explaining it again. The aim is understanding, not merely repeating a definition.

Does the assistant explain before asking questions?

Yes. For a normal learning request, it first gives a beginner-friendly explanation and then invites the learner to teach it back. It can test first when the learner explicitly requests that.

Can I study from a PDF, book, webpage, video, or image?

The skill supports learning from these materials when the host AI agent can access and interpret them. Video transcription, PDF extraction, webpage browsing, and image reading are capabilities of the host agent, not built into this instruction package.

Is Feynman Learning Companion a standalone app?

No. It is a portable set of instructions for compatible AI agents. It does not include its own AI model, chat interface, file-processing service, or persistent learner database.

Where can I get the skill?

The complete skill is available in the public GitHub repository. Download the repository and follow its installation instructions.
